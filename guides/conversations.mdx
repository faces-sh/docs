# Conversations

A **conversation** keeps the whole exchange between your agent and its faces on our side:
every message, every face's reply with its cause, every decision, and every **premise**, a
named fact your agent declares. Send only what is new on each turn; the history is put in front
of it for you, and a face's own earlier words are never taken from the request, so they cannot
be rewritten.

Conversations follow OpenAI's Conversations API: a conversation has an id, a request joins it
with `"conversation": "<id>"`, and its entries are **items** in OpenAI's Responses item format.
They were called sessions; `/v1/sessions` and the `session` field still work and name the same
thing.

## Start a conversation

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.faces.sh/v1/conversations \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{}'
```

```json
{"object": "conversation", "id": "fs_CONVERSATION_ID", "model": null, "turns": 0, "items": 0,
 "causes_visible": true, "status": "open",
 "created_at": "2026-10-28T15:47:11+00:00", "last_used_at": "2026-10-28T15:47:11+00:00",
 "expires_at": "2026-11-27T15:47:11+00:00"}
```

All fields are optional:

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `model` | A face (`alice`, `owner:alias`). The conversation then takes that face only. Left out, it takes any of your faces. |
| `causes_visible` | Whether a face sees the other faces' causes. Default `true`. |
| `items` | The first items: your messages and premises (see [Add items](#add-items)). |

## Take a turn

Add `"conversation": "<id>"` to any request with a face: [chat](/guides/chat)
(`/v1/chat/completions`, `/v1/responses`, `/v1/messages`), [decisions](/guides/decisions)
(`/v1/decisions`, `/v1/systemone`) and [causes](/guides/causes). Send only the new message.

```json
{"model": "alice@MODEL", "conversation": "fs_CONVERSATION_ID",
 "premises": [{"key": "budget_usd", "value": 50000,
               "description": "the most we can spend this year"}],
 "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Is this vendor worth a closer look?"}]}
```

The face reads the conversation so far, then your new message, and the turn is added to the
conversation: your message, then the face's reply with its `cause` and the model that wrote it.
Instructions you send (`system`, `developer`) apply to that request only and are not kept. If
you send earlier messages too, only those after the last reply in your request are kept.

A face's reply streamed or not is kept the same way. Each turn is billed as it would be without
a conversation.

## Items

Each entry is an item, in OpenAI's Responses item format, with an `id` (`ci_1`, `ci_2`, ...)
in the order it was added.

**A message.** Yours have `role: user`. A face's reply has `role: assistant`, the face's
`name`, its `cause` and the `model` that answered.

```json
{"id": "ci_7", "type": "message", "role": "assistant", "name": "alice",
 "model": "MODEL", "cause": "…", "content": "It is worth a closer look: …"}
```

**A decision.** One item of type `decision`, in the same shape whichever route asked it
(`source` says which: `decisions` or `systemone`). The `question` has its `name`, `type`
(`predicate`, `choice` or `score`), `instructions` and `options`; the `answer` has the `result`
(the probability for a predicate, the chosen value for a choice, the number for a score), the
`probabilities` and the `confidence`. It also carries the face's `cause`, the `face` and the
decision `model`. Its `call_id` is the decision's id.

```json
{"id": "ci_4", "type": "decision", "call_id": "dec_CALL_ID", "source": "decisions",
 "face": "alice", "model": "jev-1.14.0",
 "question": {"name": "planner", "type": "choice", "instructions": "Hire an outside planner?",
              "options": [{"value": "yes"}, {"value": "no"}]},
 "answer": {"result": "no", "confidence": 0.68,
            "probabilities": [{"value": "yes", "probability": 0.16}, {"value": "no", "probability": 0.84}]},
 "cause": "…"}
```

A conversation that holds decisions made before 29 October 2026 shows each of those as a
`function_call` named `decision` and its `function_call_output`, until it expires.

**A face's tool call.** When you send `tools` with a turn and the face calls one, the call is
kept as a `function_call` naming the `face` and the `model`, and the result you send back
on the next turn is kept as its `function_call_output`. On that next turn send only the
result: the conversation already holds the call it answers.

```json
{"id": "ci_3", "type": "function_call", "name": "get_weather", "call_id": "call_CALL_ID",
 "arguments": "{\"city\":\"Lisbon\"}", "face": "alice", "model": "MODEL"}
{"id": "ci_4", "type": "function_call_output", "call_id": "call_CALL_ID", "output": "18C and sunny"}
```

**A premise.** A `function_call` named `premise` holding the `key`, and its
`function_call_output` holding the `value` and its `description`.

```json
{"id": "ci_2", "type": "function_call", "name": "premise", "call_id": "prem_CALL_ID",
 "arguments": "{\"key\": \"vendor_certified\"}"}
{"id": "ci_3", "type": "function_call_output", "call_id": "prem_CALL_ID",
 "output": "{\"value\": true, \"description\": \"the vendor holds a SOC 2 report\"}"}
```

Only you add messages with `role: user` and premises. A face's replies, decisions and tool
calls are added by the turns that produce them.

## Premises

A premise is a fact your agent's decisions depend on, named by a `key` (1 to 64 letters,
digits, `_` or `-`), with a `value` and a plain `description`. The description is required
the first time a key is used, and kept after that.

Send premises with any turn as `premises`, a list of `{key, value, description}`. On
`/v1/responses` they may also be `premise` items in `input`. Or add them on their own (see
[Add items](#add-items)).

A newer value of a key replaces it from that point on. Every decision is made with the
premises in force when it was made, and the conversation keeps each value, so you can always see
what a decision was made under.

## Add items

`POST /v1/conversations/{conversation_id}/items` adds your messages and premises, at most 20
at a time, as `items` (a premise may be written `{key, value, description}`) or as `premises`.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.faces.sh/v1/conversations/fs_CONVERSATION_ID/items \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"premises": [{"key": "vendor_certified", "value": false}]}'
```

```json
{"object": "list", "data": [{"id": "ci_11", "type": "function_call", "name": "premise", …},
                            {"id": "ci_12", "type": "function_call_output", …}],
 "first_id": "ci_11", "last_id": "ci_12", "has_more": false,
 "premises": {"vendor_certified": {"value": false,
                                   "description": "the vendor holds a SOC 2 report"},
              "budget_usd": {"value": 50000,
                             "description": "the most we can spend this year"}},
 "decisions_made_under_old_values": [
   {"call_id": "dec_CALL_ID", "question": {"id": "sign", "instructions": "Sign the contract?", …},
    "choice": "no", "face": "alice",
    "changed": {"vendor_certified": {"was": true, "now": false}}}]}
```

`premises` is each key's current value. `decisions_made_under_old_values` lists every
earlier decision made while a key you just changed held a different value, so your agent can
decide which to revisit. Nothing is decided again for you.

## Several faces

A conversation opened without `model` takes any of your faces, each turn naming its face in
`model`. Each face reads its own replies as its own, and the others' as someone else's,
labelled with their name. It reads their causes too, unless `causes_visible` is `false` on
the conversation or on the turn.

## Read a conversation

`GET /v1/conversations/{conversation_id}` returns the conversation, as on creation, with
`items`, `turns` and `last_used_at` up to date.

`GET /v1/conversations/{conversation_id}/items` lists the items a page at a time, like
OpenAI's list of conversation items. **Newest first** by default.

| Parameter | What it does |
|---|---|
| `limit` | Items per page, `1` to `100`. Default `20`. |
| `order` | `desc` (newest first, the default) or `asc` (oldest first). |
| `after` | An item `id`: start the page after it. Pass the last page's `last_id` to get the next page. |
| `format` | `faces` (the default) or `openai`. |

```bash
curl "https://api.faces.sh/v1/conversations/fs_CONVERSATION_ID/items?limit=2" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY"
```

```json
{"object": "list",
 "data": [
   {"id": "ci_3", "type": "function_call_output", "call_id": "prem_CALL_ID",
    "output": "{\"value\": \"50k\", \"description\": \"the offsite budget\"}"},
   {"id": "ci_2", "type": "function_call", "name": "premise", "call_id": "prem_CALL_ID",
    "arguments": "{\"key\": \"budget\"}"}],
 "first_id": "ci_3", "last_id": "ci_2", "has_more": true,
 "premises": {"budget": {"value": "50k", "description": "the offsite budget"}}}
```

`has_more` is `true` while there are more pages. With `format=faces`, every page also carries
the `premises` in force now, all of them.

`format=openai` returns only the item types and fields OpenAI defines, so you can pass them
to anything that reads OpenAI's conversation items. Our own fields are left out: the face's
name, the model, the cause and the premises. A decision becomes a call to a `decision` tool
followed by its result. Any other `format` is a `422`.

## Compact

`POST /v1/conversations/{conversation_id}/compact` returns a summary of one face's position
over the conversation, in the face's own terms, stored under a `compaction_id`. In a
conversation open to any face, name the face in `model`.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.faces.sh/v1/conversations/fs_CONVERSATION_ID/compact \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model": "alice"}'
```

```json
{"object": "cause.compaction", "compaction_id": "fcmp_COMPACTION_ID",
 "session_id": "fs_CONVERSATION_ID", "cause": "…", "turns": 8,
 "created_at": "2026-10-28T15:49:02+00:00", "expires_at": "2026-11-27T15:49:02+00:00",
 "usage": {"input_tokens": 5210, "output_tokens": 640}}
```

Add `"compaction": "<compaction_id>"` to a later request with the same face to have its cause
read the summary. A compaction is held open like a cause: see [Speed](/guides/causes#speed).

## Close a conversation

`DELETE /v1/conversations/{conversation_id}` closes it:
`{"id": "fs_CONVERSATION_ID", "object": "conversation.deleted", "deleted": true}`. A closed
conversation takes no more turns and cannot be read. Its compactions stay usable until they
expire.

## Cost

- Opening, reading, adding items to and closing a conversation are free. A turn costs what it
  costs without one.
- **Compacting** is billed at the [cause rate](/guides/billing#current-rates) on the tokens it
  reads and writes, `usage.input_tokens + usage.output_tokens`. On Connect and Pro it counts
  against the cause [allowance](/guides/billing#allowances).
- **Using** a compaction adds its text to what the cause reads, and is billed with that cause.

Creating a conversation, adding items and compacting count against your plan's per-minute
[rate limit](/errors#rate-limits).

## Expiry

A conversation expires 30 days after it was last used, and is then deleted with everything in
it. A compaction expires 30 days after it is written. Closing your account deletes your
conversations at once.

## Errors

`detail` is a string.

| Status | When | `detail` |
|--------|------|----------|
| `404` | The conversation does not exist, has expired or been closed, or is another account's | `no such conversation, or it has expired` |
| `404` | A turn sends a `conversation` that is unknown, expired, closed or another account's | `no such conversation, or it has expired. Nothing was charged.` |
| `404` | A turn names a different face from the one the conversation was opened with | `no such conversation, or it has expired. Nothing was charged.` |
| `422` | Adding a reply or a decision as if a face wrote it | `` a client adds its own messages (`role: user`) only: a face's replies and decisions are written by the server `` |
| `422` | A premise key used for the first time, with no `description` | `` premise 'rates' is new: give its `description` (what it means, in plain words) `` |
| `422` | A premise `key` that is not 1 to 64 letters, digits, `_` or `-` | `` a premise `key` is 1-64 letters, digits, _ or - ('has space') `` |
| `422` | `premises` or `causes_visible` on a turn with no `conversation` | `` `premises` and `causes_visible` belong to a conversation: send its id as `conversation` `` |
| `422` | `conversation` and `session` both sent, with different ids | `` send `conversation` (or its old name `session`), not both `` |
| `422` | `causes_visible` that is not `true` or `false` | `` `causes_visible` is true or false `` |
| `422` | Compacting a conversation open to any face, without `model` | `` this conversation takes any face: name the one to compact in `model` `` |
| `402` | A turn or a compaction with no credit left | See [Billing](/guides/billing). |
| `403` | The API key may not use the face | See [Errors](/errors). |
| `429` | The per-minute rate limit, or the cause allowance is used and `api_fallback` is off | Names the limit. |
