# Causes (removed)

`POST /v1/causes` is removed. It answers `422`, and nothing is charged:

```json
{"detail": "/v1/causes is removed: a face's cause is not returned. Steer a decision with POST /v1/decisions and `model: \"@face:maestro-1/<decision model>\"`, or ask the face's options with POST /v1/choices and `model: \"@face:maestro-1\"`. Nothing was charged."}
```

A face's cause still steers every answer it gives: the reply on [chat](/guides/chat), the
numbers on [decisions](/guides/decisions). It stays on the server and is never returned. To
have a face's view in your own pipeline:

- [chat](/guides/chat) with the face on the model you already use, `@alice:maestro-1/gpt-5.4`,
  through any OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible client;
- or ask it what it would do with [choices](/guides/choices), `@alice:maestro-1`.
