# Compiled Faces

A face is a causal model compiled from source texts. Prompted, it returns a
[cause](/guides/causes): the principal *why* behind its response in that context.

---

## What compiling does

Each document or thread you compile becomes part of the face. Delete it, and what it
contributed goes with it.

A face's cost per request does not depend on how much you have compiled into it.

---

## Why faces compose

Compiled faces can be combined with boolean operators (union, intersection,
difference), and the result is still a usable face. See
[synthetic faces](/guides/synthetic-faces).

---

## What makes a good source text

- **First-person material is strongest.** Interviews, journals, essays and sent
  messages carry voice as well as content.
- **Third-person material works** for biography and description; set
  `perspective=third-person` so it is read as being *about* the person rather than
  *by* them.
- **Volume helps, but distinctiveness helps more.** Ten pages where someone argues a
  position beat a hundred pages of logistics.
- **Declare what a document is** when you upload it, with `medium`. It is required: a
  document cannot be saved without one. See
  [Compile](/guides/compile#declaring-what-a-document-is-required-to-save).
